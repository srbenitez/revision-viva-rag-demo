The flow has four steps. First the person sets the question and the inclusion criteria. Second a program deduplicates in code. It compares the normalized D O I and the similar titles. This part is not decided by the model. Third the artificial intelligence suggests including or excluding each study. It always cites the abstract and gives a short reason. Fourth the person reviews and decides. The decision is signed with their name and the date. The model proposes. We decide and we keep a record. Each step leaves a trace that can be audited.
