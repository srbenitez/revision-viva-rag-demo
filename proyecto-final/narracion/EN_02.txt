A bibliographic review starts ageing the day it is delivered. New studies appear every week. Searching again and removing duplicates by hand almost never happens. There is no time. Let us look at a concrete case. We bring a new batch from Scopus. An article we already had comes back in. Its D O I identifier is written as a web address. If nobody notices, it slips in twice. This ageing is silent and easy to miss. In this way the review loses trust.
